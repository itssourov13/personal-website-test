# Brand Identity

> ✅ Approved baseline direction. Working persona is a placeholder (A-001) — replace details via the customization checklist; the _direction_ stays unless the owner overrides it.

## 1. Positioning statement

> For **product teams that demand uncommon quality**, Sourov Mondol is the **independent product designer & engineer** who ships design and code as one craft — bringing agency-level polish and startup-level speed in a single senior hand.

- **Category:** Independent product designer & front-end engineer (consultant)
- **Differentiator:** Design and engineering under one roof, delivered with editorial craft
- **Proof:** case studies with measurable outcomes + a site that visibly walks the talk

## 2. Brand personality (what the brand sounds and feels like)

**The Quiet Expert — confident, warm, precise.** Think a well-made mechanical watch: no logo shout, exact tolerances, obvious quality to anyone who looks closely.

| Trait axis    | Lean toward                            | Avoid                                 |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Confidence    | Calm authority, evidence-based claims  | Hype, superlatives, "10x growth guru" |
| Warmth        | Direct, human, first-person voice      | Corporate we-speak, jargon            |
| Verve         | Playful detail in microcopy and motion | Filler, puns everywhere, clutter      |
| Polish        | Restraint, whitespace, precision       | Decoration for its own sake           |
| Accessibility | Plain language, generous contrast      | Insider phrasing                      |

## 3. Brand voice & tone

- **First person singular** ("I design…", "Here's how I work") — it's a personal site.
- **Present, active, specific.** "I redesigned the checkout flow" → "Redesigned a 9-step checkout into 3 screens and raised completion by 22%."
- **No corporate filler**: no "synergy", "empower", "world-class solutions". If a word adds nothing, cut it.
- **Tone of voice guide** (full version lives in `04-strategy/content-strategy.md` §6):
  - Home: direct, confident, warm
  - Case studies: analytical, honest (include what didn't work)
  - Notes: curious, opinionated, generous
  - Contact: helpful, human, fast
  - Errors/404: calm, slightly playful — never alarming

## 4. Logo / wordmark (v1 direction)

- **Primary lockup:** lowercase wordmark — `sourov mondol` with a copper full stop: **"sourov mondol."**
- Typographic only (Fraunces, ~600 weight, optical size large). No icon required; the full stop is the mark.
- **Favicon / app icon:** the copper full stop on the paper background (`--accent` dot, 44 px padding rules per `01-brand/design-guidelines.md`), and a dark-mode variant.
- **Clear space:** ≥ the height of the full stop on all sides. **Minimum size:** wordmark ≥ 120 px wide; below that use the dot mark.
- **Do not:** outline, gradient the wordmark, animate it every load, place it on photos without scrim, or add taglines to the lockup.

## 5. Name & grammar rules

| Item              | Rule                                                           | Example                     |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Personal name     | As the owner spells it; never shortened in first mentions      | "Sourov Mondol"             |
| Site/domain       | lowercase, no www in copy                                      | sourovmondol.studio         |
| Company-ish label | Avoid; the person IS the brand                                 | —                           |
| Client names      | First mention full; anonymize where NDA ("a fintech scale-up") | "Finta (name changed)"      |
| Numbers/metrics   | specific, honest, rounded where appropriate                    | "raised completion by ~22%" |

## 6. Visual identity pillars (details in `design-concept.md` + `design-guidelines.md`)

1. **Warm paper & ink** — off-white background, warm black text; feels printed, not digital.
2. **Editorial typography** — Fraunces display serif (character, soul) + Inter UI sans (clarity, neutrality).
3. **One accent — burnt copper** — used sparingly: links, focus states, the full stop, key numbers. Copper reads "premium, crafted, warm" and pairs with the palette.
4. **Space as luxury** — generous page/section rhythm; content breathes.
5. **Honest imagery** — real project screenshots and photography, consistent grading (warm, soft-light); no stock corporate hands-shaking.

## 7. Brand words → have at hand

craft · precision · shipped · measurable · senior · independent · editorial · calm · fast · human · honest · deliberate

**Anti-words:** innovative ✗ synergize ✗ passionate about ✗ gurus ✗ ninjas ✗ world-class ✗ cutting-edge ✗ solutions (unqualified) ✗

- Bio line everywhere: _"Independent product designer & engineer. I turn ambitious products into shipped, measurable design."_ + site link.
- Consistent avatar (the dot mark or a portrait per owner's choice).
