## What it is

Most sites only _read_ `prefers-reduced-motion` from the OS. This is a
small widget testing the other direction — a visible, persistent toggle
that lets someone override the system setting per-site, for the case
where their OS-level preference doesn't match what they want on this
one page.

## Why I built it

A few people mentioned wanting motion on a device where they'd set
reduced-motion system-wide for unrelated reasons (battery, a shared
family device). Curious whether a per-site override control makes sense
as a pattern or is just extra UI nobody uses.
